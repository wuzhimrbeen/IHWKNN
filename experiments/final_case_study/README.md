# Final-model case study

`generate_final_top15.py` fits the final IHWKNN configuration to each complete observed matrix and exports the top 15 previously unrecorded pairs for six datasets with reliable identifier mappings. `build_top15_workbook.mjs` formats the review workbook.

The case study is hypothesis-generating. A treatment-related keyword or indication overlap indicates semantic relevance only; it does not confirm a drug-disease association, therapeutic efficacy, or clinical utility. SCMFDDL and TLHGBI were excluded from pair-level review because reliable identifier mapping was unavailable.

The reviewed workbook distributed with the supplement is the authoritative record used by the revised manuscript.
